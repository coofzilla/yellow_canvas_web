A few quick
answers.

Practical help for the current iPhone and iPad app.

How will the App Store purchase work?+

YellowCanvas 1.0 is being prepared as a paid App Store download. You purchase it through Apple before downloading the app. There is no free trial, recurring subscription, or in-app purchase to unlock its features.

How many videos can I use?+

Review one video on its own, or add a second to compare two perspectives. Each canvas supports a maximum of two videos. Use + to add another, or Replace video in Project options to change the selected clip.

How do I synchronize two videos?+

Use the individual alignment controls to find the same event in each video, then tap Sync these frames. You choose the matching moment. To adjust a saved synchronization, open the alignment controls again. Frame stepping uses the selected video as its timing reference.

What can I draw or point with?+

Tap the pencil to enter Draw. Use the native drawing palette for saved annotations, or choose Laser for a temporary trail that fades after about one second. Drawings are static overlays; they do not track a moving subject. Undo, redo, and clear apply to the selected video’s saved ink.

Why did the playback controls disappear?+

Playback controls fade automatically so you can see your footage. Tap a video to show or hide them during review. In Draw, touching or drawing on a video reveals them again without stopping playback; they fade after input stops. Scrubbing and held frame-step controls remain visible while you use them.

How do I move or zoom a video?+

Pan and pinch while reviewing. In Draw, use two fingers to pan or zoom so one finger or Apple Pencil can draw. Gentle snapping near pane centers and edges helps with small adjustments. Keep dragging to move freely, or use Reset view in Project options to return the selected video to its fitted position.

Where are my projects saved?+

Projects and imported video copies are stored inside YellowCanvas on this device. Completed imports work offline. YellowCanvas does not provide cross-device project sync. Your device’s iCloud or computer backups may include app data, depending on your Apple settings.

Will removing a video delete my Photos original?+

No. Removing or replacing a clip affects its copy and drawing inside that canvas. Removing the final clip returns the same canvas to See the difference so you can add videos again. Deleting a saved project removes its local videos and drawings. Your original videos remain in Photos.

A Photos import is slow or won’t finish. What should I try?+

A video stored only in iCloud Photos may first need to download through Apple. Check your connection, allow the original to download in Photos, and confirm that your device has enough available storage for a local copy. You can cancel an import and retry. Send the steps and any error message if the issue continues.

Can I export a comparison or import from Files?+

This version imports videos from Photos and saves review projects locally. Rendered video export, Files import, automatic motion synchronization, and cross-device project sync are not currently included.

How do I delete my data or make a privacy request?+

Use Saved projects to delete a canvas and its local video copies and drawings. Deleting the app using iOS’s Delete App option removes its remaining data from that device. Backups and Photos originals are managed separately through Apple. For support correspondence or other information under the developer’s control, email yellowdevjay@gmail.com.

Something went wrong?

Help us see
what happened.

When you contact support, include:

  • What you were trying to do and what happened instead.
  • Your iPhone or iPad model and iOS or iPadOS version.
  • Your YellowCanvas version and the steps to reproduce the issue.
  • The exact error message, if there is one.

A screenshot can help, but crop out private footage and personal information. You do not need to send the full video to start a support request. If you use TestFlight, you can also send feedback through Apple’s TestFlight app.

Purchase or billing question?

Apple handles App Store payments, receipts, and refund requests. Contact Apple for help with an App Store transaction; contact yellowdevjay@gmail.com for app problems. Never send payment-card details, passwords, or verification codes to support.
