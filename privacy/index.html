Privacy policy · Effective November 11, 2026
Operator: Jeric Hernandez

YellowCanvas lets you review one video or compare two, synchronize playback and draw on your footage. Your projects are stored locally. YellowCanvas does not upload your videos or drawings to a server operated by us.

Videos and information on your device

When you select videos through Apple's Photos picker, YellowCanvas imports independent copies into its own storage. It uses only the videos you select, rather than requesting access to your entire photo library. Your originals remain in Photos.

Local project data includes those video copies and filenames, video duration and dimensions, project names and identifiers, creation and modification dates, synchronization and playback settings, drawings, and your last-opened project preference. This information lets the app restore your canvases and play your videos together.

Accounts, tracking and network services

YellowCanvas does not require an account and has no app-operated backend, advertising, third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not sell your personal information or use your footage for advertising or AI training.

A selected video stored in iCloud Photos may need to be downloaded by Apple during import. Apple manages that service under your Apple account and settings.

Backups and deleting your projects

Projects remain in the app until you remove or replace their contents, delete a project, or delete the app. Deleting a project removes its local video copies and drawings without deleting the original videos in Photos. Using iOS's Delete App option removes the app's remaining data from that device.

Your device's iCloud or computer backups may include YellowCanvas data, depending on your Apple settings. YellowCanvas does not provide its own project synchronization service. Backup copies and iCloud Photos originals are managed separately through Apple and your backup settings.

App Store purchases

Purchases of YellowCanvas through Apple's App Store are processed by Apple under its own terms and privacy practices. Apple manages payment processing, receipts and refund requests. The YellowCanvas app does not receive your payment-card details. YellowCanvas does not include a free trial, an in-app subscription or an in-app purchase system.

TestFlight beta testing

If you install YellowCanvas through TestFlight, Apple automatically collects crash and usage information and shares it with the developer. This can include app and device information, installation and session details, and crash reports. Your name and email may be included for email invitations; they are not displayed to us when you join solely through a public link.

Feedback you choose to send through TestFlight can include comments, screenshots and diagnostic information. Screenshots or attachments may show your footage or other personal information. We use beta information to investigate issues, respond to feedback and improve YellowCanvas, and do not share it with other third parties.

We keep any copies of beta information under our control only as needed to investigate and resolve issues or meet applicable obligations. Apple controls retention in its own services. Read Apple's TestFlight privacy information for details.

Support and privacy requests

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to respond and resolve your request, and retain correspondence only as needed for that purpose or applicable obligations. Send only the information needed to explain the issue.

For access, correction or deletion requests concerning information we control, contact yellowdevjay@gmail.com. We cannot access or remotely delete the projects stored on your device. You can manage those projects in the app; Apple manages its own service records and backups.

This policy website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no analytics scripts, advertising cookies or tracking pixels. GitHub logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security, including for visitors who are not signed in. See GitHub's hosting documentation and privacy statement.

Policy updates

We will update this page and its effective date when YellowCanvas's data practices change. This policy describes the current app, its TestFlight beta and this website.
